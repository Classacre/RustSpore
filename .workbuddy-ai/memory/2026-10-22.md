## Creature skin generation (task #25, closed)

Added `crates/spore-core/src/skin.rs`: rasterise a creature's own UVs and paint them from
its three-colour paint palette. `AssetSource::creature_texture` now returns the shipped skin
when there is one and the generated atlas otherwise, so all 1,022 creatures render with a
diffuse map. New mode `--creature-skin <package> <instance> [out.png] [--force]`.

161 tests pass (dbpf 20, spore-core 141).

### What the measurements say

- 656 of 1,022 creatures ship no skin; 366 do.
- 183 of the 366 have a resolvable mesh, so the two paths can be compared.
- The shipped alpha mask lies **inside** the generated one: IoU equals the shipped mask's
  own area fraction to two decimals on 5 of 5 (0.098/0.092, 0.193/0.186, 0.268/0.269,
  0.271/0.275, 0.348/0.349). The rasteriser lands on the artist's islands.
- The palette is a colour *set*, not a flat palette for the shipped skin. Over 40 creatures
  with both: all three colours appear within 0.08 RGB somewhere in the skin for 30, at
  least one for 37, but only a median **14%** of opaque pixels are that close (p10 1%,
  p90 30%). The earlier "every palette colour appears, 5 sampled" claim was too strong as
  an account of what the palette is for.
- Creature UVs live in one tile with negative `v` (`u 0.005..0.998`, `v -0.998..-0.006`),
  0 triangles straddle a seam after the whole-tile shift.

### Generator lessons that cost time

- A per-triangle hash averaged over 28k triangles converges to a constant: the pattern must
  be a function of the texel's world position, not of the triangle.
- Interpolate position/normal barycentrically per texel. Using the triangle centroid paints
  one flat patch per triangle and shows as facets at 512.
- Any absolute threshold on the marking field either marks nothing or two thirds of the
  body: the field's distribution is skewed and its amplitude is small. Rank-based masking
  (top 30% by value among back texels) plus a blurred mask is the version that works.
- Trilinear value noise is C0, so its iso-contours crease at lattice planes and read as
  straight-edged patches. A sum of plane waves has no creases.
- Marking softness must come from blurring the mask, not from a ramp on the field.
- `nearest palette colour` is a harsh metric: a 40% mix still counts as the base colour.
  It is still the right metric for "did the third colour actually get used", because it
  reports 0 when the accent never wins.

### Tooling trap that produced a false result

A Python heredoc wrote instance lists with CRLF; `u64::from_str_radix("...\r", 16)` returns
Err and the tool fell back to instance 0, so a 366-creature survey reported "all 366 have no
mesh". It was an artifact of the loop, not a finding. Strip `\r` (`tr -d '\r'`) before
feeding lists to the binary, or check for `no creature 0000000000000000` in the output.

## New tools

- `tools/creature_paint_probe.py` header field distributions across all 1,022 creatures.
- `tools/creature_skin_probe.py` finds a creature's skin rasters, decodes the largest,
  writes a PNG (own writer, no PIL) and reports opacity and colour counts.
- `tools/png_read.py` RGBA8 PNG reader with all five filters, for comparing generated and
  shipped atlases without PIL.
