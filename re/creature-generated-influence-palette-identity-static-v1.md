## What the numeric join establishes

A row count at most 256 and an index at most 255 avoid low-byte truncation; the original reader's 256-row cap is not a guarantee for arbitrary alternate objects. The generated slot can be compared to the corresponding row ordinal only under all of these conditions; it is not evidence that one part exclusively owns the position.
